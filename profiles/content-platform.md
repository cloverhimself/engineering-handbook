# Content Platform Profile

Use for publishing, media, community, course, article, or user-generated content systems.

Priorities:
- separate authoring, publishing, moderation, discovery, and delivery concerns;
- define draft, scheduled, published, archived, and deleted states explicitly where relevant;
- model ownership and editorial permissions server-side;
- sanitize and validate user-generated content;
- plan media storage, transformations, CDN delivery, and lifecycle costs;
- use pagination for feeds and libraries;
- design search separately from canonical content storage when requirements justify it;
- preserve moderation/audit history for sensitive changes;
- consider abuse, spam, copyright/reporting workflows, and rate limits;
- avoid introducing a search engine, queue, or media pipeline until workload/features require them.
