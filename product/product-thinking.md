# Product Thinking

Before building a feature, define the user, problem, desired outcome, success signal, constraints, and smallest useful workflow.

Distinguish:
- must-have requirements;
- desirable improvements;
- speculative future features.

Do not build speculative complexity into V1 merely because it may be useful someday.

For each important flow consider empty, loading, success, validation-error, permission-denied, dependency-failure, retry, and recovery states.

Destructive or financially consequential actions should be explicit and understandable to the user.
