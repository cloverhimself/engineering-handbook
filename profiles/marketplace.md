# Marketplace Profile

Use for products connecting buyers and sellers/providers.

Priorities:
- distinguish platform, seller/provider, and buyer ownership clearly;
- model listings, availability, orders/bookings, commissions, payouts, refunds, disputes, and moderation separately;
- never conflate customer payment with seller payout;
- snapshot fees and commercial terms at transaction time;
- enforce resource ownership and role checks server-side;
- design onboarding and verification states explicitly;
- support dispute and reversal workflows;
- maintain audit trails for administrative intervention;
- consider abuse, fraud, fake listings, collusion, and account takeover risks;
- ensure search/listing pagination and filtering can scale independently of transactional records;
- document how platform fees, processor fees, taxes, and seller proceeds are calculated and reconciled.
